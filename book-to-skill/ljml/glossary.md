# Glossary

**对象媒介 (Object Media)** — media treated as externally existing, objectively measurable artifacts, independent of the subject. The default but uncritical presupposition of mainstream communication studies. (Ch 1)

**现象媒介 (Phenomenal Media)** — media as they appear in lived bodily experience: "显现在本己身体经验中的媒介，是一种被体验的媒介". The originary ground of object media. (Ch 1, 3)

**具身化 (Embodiment)** — the thesis that cognition and communication are fundamentally bodily; media are extensions of the phenomenal body, not objects added to a disembodied subject. (Ch 2)

**元媒介 (Meta-Medium)** — the body as the most originary medium, the medium of all media: "身体是在世界上存在的媒介物". (Ch 2)

**现象身体 (Phenomenal Body)** — body as lived from the first-person perspective, neither pure extended substance nor pure thinking substance but their original synthesis. (Ch 2)

**补余的逻辑 (Logic of Complementarity)** — (斯蒂格勒) the organism's programs extend into artificial programs; the extension is formed in the process of extending — no interiority precedes extension. (Ch 2)

**技术意向性 (Technical Intentionality)** — (唐·伊德) technologies inherit and transform bodily intentionality; technology can occupy the "of" position in consciousness-of-something. (Ch 2, 3)

**含混性 (Ambiguity)** — media are neither pure material carrier nor pure meaning; the double negation that yields a normative double affirmation. (Ch 3)

**意向性 (Intentionality)** — (胡塞尔) all consciousness is "consciousness of something"; media inherit the body's directedness toward the world. (Ch 3)

**象征性 (Symbolicity)** — media's capacity to establish mutual reference and equivalence between different meanings, like money for commodities. (Ch 3)

**表达性 (Expressivity)** — media's capacity not merely to refer to reality but to create new meaning; 媒介即意义，媒介即存有. (Ch 3)

**情境性 (Situatedness)** — media are always generated in concrete cognitive/communicative situations; there is no media outside situation. (Ch 3)

**普遍性 (Universality)** — media presuppose a universal horizon of world-access that transcends any specific situation. (Ch 3)

**身体的厚度 (Thickness of the Body)** — the irreducible distance/opacity of the body that makes media both possible and necessary; complete transparency would abolish media. (Ch 3)

**主体间性 (Intersubjectivity)** — the mutual directedness between embodied subjects that grounds the possibility of communication. (Ch 3)

**现象学还原 (Phenomenological Reduction)** — the method of suspending the natural/objective attitude to return to lived experience as it appears. (Ch 1, 4)

**离身传播困境 (Disembodied Communication Dilemma)** — the problem created by ignoring lived perceptual experience in communication research. (Ch 1)
