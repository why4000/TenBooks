## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 对象媒介 vs 现象媒介 (Object Media vs Phenomenal Media)
- **Use when** analyzing how media research conflates media-objects with lived media experience.
- Object media treats media as external, measurable, objectively existing artifacts. Phenomenal media is media as it appears in lived bodily experience — "显现在本己身体经验中的媒介".
- **Key insight**: 对象媒介是现象媒介的理论化产物；现象媒介先于对象媒介，是其意义根源。
- **Anti-pattern**: Equating "媒介" with "媒介物" leads to "万物皆媒" which abolishes media studies itself.

### 媒介的具身化 (Embodiment of Media)
- **Use when** questioning disembodied models of communication.
- 身体是"元媒介" — the most originary medium, the medium of all media. Media are extensions of the phenomenal body, not objects added to a pre-existing subject.
- **Key insight**: "身体是在世界上存在的媒介物" — to have a body is to be embedded in an environment. Media originate from the body's need to overcome its limitations (distance, time, perception).
- **Complementarity logic (斯蒂格勒)**: 外延之物恰恰在其外延过程中形成 — there is no interiority before the extension.

### 现象媒介的五重特征 (Five Features of Phenomenal Media)
1. **含混性与规范性** — media are neither pure material carriers nor pure meaning; they are the original synthesis of both. The double-negation ("既非...也非...") yields the double-affirmation ("既是...又是...").
2. **意向性与对象性** — all consciousness is consciousness-of-something. Media inherit the body's intentional directedness toward the world. 对象媒介 is the deposit of 身体意向 into a specific object.
3. **象征性与表达性** — media not only symbolize (refer to) reality but express (create) reality. 媒介即意义，媒介即存有.
4. **情境性与普遍性** — media are always generated in concrete situations, yet presuppose a universal horizon of world-access. The body's "thickness" (厚度) is irreducible — complete transparency would abolish media.
5. **主体性与主体间性** — media actively constitute reality (not just reflect it); intersubjectivity grounds the possibility of communication.

### 现象学还原方法 (Phenomenological Reduction)
- **Use when** need to trace a constituted object back to its constitutive lived experience.
- Step: suspend the "objective" attitude → return to the lived body's original relation with the world → describe phenomena as they appear.
- Applied here: from 对象媒介 (constituted) back to 现象媒介 (constituting).
