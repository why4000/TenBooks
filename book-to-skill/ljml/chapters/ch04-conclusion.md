# Chapter 4: 结语——"现象媒介"之意蕴

## Core Idea
The disclosure of 现象媒介 lays a new existential-ontological foundation for media and media studies, guiding researchers to rethink the relations between media and humans, media and nature, and media and society.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **存在论转向 (Ontological Turn)**: shifting media studies from epistemology (how do we know media?) to ontology (how do media constitute being?).
  - When to use: when designing media research that goes beyond content-effects models.
- **媒介即生存方式**: media are not just tools but the very way humans exist in the world. A media history is a human survival history.

## Key Concepts
- **媒介学的存在论基础**: the ground that media studies has been missing — media as constitutive of human existence, not merely instruments.
- **媒介与人/自然/社会的关系**: the three axes that the phenomenal-media framework reconfigures.

## Mental Models
- Think of media as the "infrastructure of existence" — like roads, but for meaning.
- The phenomenal turn does not abolish object-media research; it re-founds it on its proper ground.

## Anti-patterns
- **在客观和科学的口号中错失理解媒介的密码**: pursuing quantitative objectivity while ignoring the subjective-perceptual constitution of the very object being measured.

## Key Takeaways
1. 现象媒介 provides a new ontological foundation for media studies.
2. Media studies must move beyond the object/phenomenon dichotomy.
3. The phenomenal turn reconfigures how we understand media's relations to humans, nature, and society.
