# Chapter 1: 对象媒介与现象媒介

## Core Idea
Mainstream communication studies uncritically presupposes media as objective objects. This presupposition must be overturned: media are not external objects but phenomena constituted in lived bodily experience. 对象媒介 is a theoretical derivative of the more originary 现象媒介.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **对象媒介 (Object Media)**: media treated as externally existing, objectively measurable artifacts independent of the subject.
  - When to use: when critiquing the default objectivist stance in media studies.
  - How: identify where a scholar conflates "媒介" with "媒介物".
- **现象媒介 (Phenomenal Media)**: media as they appear in first-person bodily experience — "显现在本己身体经验中的媒介，是一种被体验的媒介".
  - When to use: when seeking the existential ground of media.
  - How: perform phenomenological reduction to trace object-media back to lived experience.

## Key Concepts
- **离身传播困境**: the problem of disembodied communication — ignoring lived perceptual experience.
- **拟态环境 (李普曼)**: Lippmann's "pseudo-environment"; the article argues this distinction (拟态/客观) is itself a product of objectifying media.
- **对距离的匆忙消除 (海德格尔)**: the illusion that shrinking physical distance brings nearness; psychological distance is phenomenal, not physical.

## Mental Models
- Think of 对象媒介 as the "frozen" or "crystallized" form of 现象媒介 — the way a crystal condenses from a solution.
- "鱼无视水" — we are unaware of phenomenal media precisely because they constitute our experiential environment.

## Anti-patterns
- **万物皆媒**: if everything is media, nothing is media; this abolishes media studies.
- **把媒介等同于媒介物**: reduces media to physical carriers, missing their phenomenal ground.

## Key Takeaways
1. 对象媒介 is a theoretical construction; 现象媒介 is its lived ground.
2. The objective/subjective split in media research creates pseudo-problems.
3. Understanding media requires shifting from object-thinking to phenomenal thinking.
